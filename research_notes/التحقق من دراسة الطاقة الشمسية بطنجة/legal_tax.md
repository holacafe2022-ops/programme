# Moroccan legal and tax framework for residential rooftop solar self-generation (Law 82-21, Decree 2-25-100), status as of Nov 2026, villa in Tangier

Research date: 7 Nov 2026. Primary text read in full: Law 82-21 (French BO 7400, via ANRE PDF). The French text of Decree 2-25-100 was NOT reachable (the SGG Arabic BO 7489 PDF failed to load), so decree article numbers below come from a law-firm analysis (LEXT Avocats, 19 May 2026) and from press reports.

### Takeaway
Confirmed. Decree 2-25-100 is dated 5 March 2026 and was published in BO n°7489 (an installer gives the date as 9 March 2026). Press and installer sources give 9 June 2026 as the date it took effect. I could not access the Fidal note itself, but its LinkedIn excerpt matches.

## Q2. Thresholds: below 11 kW declaration vs 11 kW–5 MW connection agreement; meaning of "puissance" (DC kWc or AC kW)

### Takeaway
The thresholds are right: declaration below 11 kW on BT, connection agreement from 11 kW to 5 MW, authorisation from 5 MW. The study's description of the "simple declaration" is too light. According to LEXT's reading of the decree, a grid-connected BT declaration still involves a connection diagram, a check and reservation of hosting capacity by the network operator, and a signed connection convention (convention de raccordement) before installation (decree arts. 6–10). No official text or guidance found says whether "puissance" means DC kWc (panels) or AC kW (inverter). The law uses "puissance nominale" (arts. 7, 11) and reports capacities "en kilowatts ou en kilovoltampères" (art. 22).

### Cited Findings
- Law art. 4: an installation below a regulatory threshold that is connected to the self-consumption installation, "qu'elle soit préalablement raccordée au réseau basse tension ou destinée à y être raccordée", requires a declaration to the "gestionnaire du réseau de distribution d'électricité concerné ... avant d'entamer la réalisation ou l'exploitation". Art. 5: connection agreement from that threshold up to 5 MW (BT or MT). Art. 6: authorisation from 5 MW. Art. 3: off-grid installations (self-consumption site not connected to the national grid) are declared to "l'administration" — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- Law art. 11: the power of a site with several installations is the sum of "la puissance nominale de chacune". Art. 22: operators report each installation's power "en kilowatts ou en kilovoltampères" — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- Decree sets the threshold at 11 kW (declaration below 11 kW on BT; agreement ≥11 kW to 5 MW; authorisation ≥5 MW), with references to decree arts. 5, 11 and 18 — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- BT declaration regime: "le décret impose un schéma de raccordement, une vérification de la capacité d'accueil, une réservation de capacité et la signature d'une convention de raccordement avant la réalisation de l'installation (Décret n°2-25-100, art. 6 à 10)". The operator reserves capacity in order of receipt of complete files (arts. 7–8). At the operating stage, the applicant must show title to, or the right to use, the installation (art. 9) — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- Connection agreement regime (11 kW–5 MW): the file includes a connection diagram, technical characteristics, schedule and an annual consumption report (art. 12). Study fees are paid, followed by provisional and then definitive reservation (arts. 13–14). The technical study is "aux frais du demandeur" (art. 27) — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- Médias24 says no fees are charged below 11 kW, and describes two sub-regimes: a declaration to the energy ministry if off-grid, and a "régime d'accord de raccordement" with the SRM, which checks hosting capacity first-come-first-served, if connected — [Médias24, 18 Mar 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/03/18/electricite-apres-trois-ans-dattente-le-maroc-debloque-enfin-lautoproduction-1645705/). The law (art. 4) calls the connected <11 kW case a "déclaration" to the DSO. Médias24 is probably describing the declaration plus connection-convention steps loosely.
- Contradicting source: Barlamane (1 Jul 2026, relaying a SurgePV guide) says "déclaration préalable jusqu'à 2 MW; 2–50 MW accord de l'ANRE; >50 MW ministère". This contradicts the law (art. 5–6: 5 MW, connection agreement from the network operator) and should be treated as unreliable — [Barlamane](https://barlamane.com/fr/le-maroc-ouvre-lautoproduction-solaire-aux-entreprises-plafonne-les-injections-a-20-et-maintient-en-attente-le-regime-residentiel-de-basse-tension/)
- An installer's own worked example labels a 10 kWc villa as "Régime : Accord de raccordement" while its table puts ≤11 kW under declaration. This shows how unsettled practice is — [Wevmade](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026)
- Hosting capacity for distribution set by ANRE at 691 MW across all DSOs (Médias24); ANRE decision n°01/26 approves 2026–2030 hosting capacities (LEXT) — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/03/18/electricite-apres-trois-ans-dattente-le-maroc-debloque-enfin-lautoproduction-1645705/); [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)

### Inferences
- "Puissance nominale" is not defined in the law or in any source found. The law's use of "kW ou kVA" points to the AC output rating (inverter or grid-side), which is common grid-code practice, but this is not confirmed. A cautious design keeps both DC kWc and AC kW below 11, or gets the SRM's written position, because art. 9 requires prior consent for any change, and art. 29 penalises operating above the threshold without an agreement. A villa with, say, 12 kWc DC on a 10 kW inverter sits in a legal grey zone.
- "Simple declaration" is misleading for a grid-connected villa. Expect a capacity check and reservation, a connection convention, a smart meter installed by the operator, and possibly a wait if local BT hosting capacity is full.

### Gaps
- No ANRE, ministry or SRM guidance found on DC vs AC power, or on whether the threshold uses kW or kVA.
- Response times for the BT declaration could not be confirmed from the decree. Wevmade's "récépissé sous 1 mois" and "15 à 30 jours" for agreements are unverified installer claims.

## Q3. Is a declaration needed for zero-export (anti-injection) systems?

### Takeaway
Yes, under the law. Art. 4 covers any installation below the threshold that is connected to a self-consumption installation already connected, or to be connected, to the BT grid. It does not depend on whether energy is exported. A grid-tied villa with an anti-injection inverter still has to declare to the network operator. Only a truly off-grid site (art. 3) is declared to the administration instead, and it also needs no smart meter (art. 18).

### Cited Findings
- Art. 4 triggers on connection of the self-consumption installation to the BT grid ("préalablement raccordée au réseau basse tension ou destinée à y être raccordée"), not on injection. Art. 3 makes off-grid installations declarable to the administration. Art. 18 requires a smart meter for every installation except art. 3 (off-grid) ones. Art. 28 fines anyone who operates "sans procéder aux déclarations prévues aux articles 3 et 4" — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- LEXT summary: a declaration applies to off-grid installations and to installations connected or to be connected to BT below 11 kW — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- Barlamane/SurgePV (mid-2026): "les particuliers peuvent installer des systèmes hors réseau ou réservés à leur propre consommation, mais le comptage avec rémunération des excédents n'est pas disponible" — [Barlamane](https://barlamane.com/fr/le-maroc-ouvre-lautoproduction-solaire-aux-entreprises-plafonne-les-injections-a-20-et-maintient-en-attente-le-regime-residentiel-de-basse-tension/)

### Inferences
- Zero-export does not exempt a grid-tied villa from declaring. Some installers may tell clients otherwise; that has no legal basis in the text.

### Gaps
- No official guidance or SRM practice note found that addresses anti-injection systems specifically.

## Q4. Surplus sale: 20% cap, ANRE decision 04/26 values and scope, BT tariff, "0.85 MAD/kWh"

### Takeaway
The 20% cap is in art. 12 of the law. ANRE decision n°04/26 sets 0.21 MAD/kWh peak and 0.18 MAD/kWh off-peak for 1 Mar 2026 – 28 Feb 2027, and covers HT/THT and the MT distribution networks only. ANRE says the BT tariff will be set later. No BT surplus tariff was found as of Nov 2026, and no source supports a "0.85 MAD/kWh" credit or any net-metering.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 12: the self-producer may sell "une proportion n'excédant pas 20 % de la production annuelle"; the tariff is set by ANRE; the cap may be revised at the self-producer's request under regulatory conditions — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- ANRE (19 Feb 2026): "21 centimes le kilowattheure en Heures de Pointe et à 18 centimes ... en Heures hors Pointe"; period 1 Mar 2026 – 28 Feb 2027; single tariff for all production installations; "s'applique au réseau de haute et très haute tension ainsi qu'aux réseaux électriques de moyenne tension de la distribution. Le tarif applicable au réseau de la basse tension sera fixé par l'ANRE après la mise en place du cadre réglementaire et technique adéquat." Decision PDF: "décision n°04/26 fixant le tarif de l'excédent ... lois n°40-19 et n°82-21" — [ANRE](https://anre.ma/actualites/lanre-fixe-le-tarif-de-lexcedent-de-lenergie-electrique-pour-la-periode-de-regulation-allant-du-1er-mars-2026-au-28-fevrier-2027/) ([decision PDF](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Decision_ANRE_-Tarif_Exceident-Version-FRF.pdf))
- Other ANRE 2026 decisions: TURT 6.85 c/kWh (dec. 02/26), MT TURD 6.07 c/kWh (dec. 03/26), system services 6.81 c/kWh — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- Mid-2026: "la basse tension (BT), qui couvre l'essentiel des foyers, reste privée de barème"; "le régime de basse tension n'est pas encore appliqué" — [Barlamane, 1 Jul 2026](https://barlamane.com/fr/le-maroc-ouvre-lautoproduction-solaire-aux-entreprises-plafonne-les-injections-a-20-et-maintient-en-attente-le-regime-residentiel-de-basse-tension/)
- Installer expectation: BT tariff "horizon 2027"; Morocco uses net-billing, not net-metering — [Wevmade](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026)
- The industry is waiting for a further decree on how the 20% cap can be revised — [Médias24, 18 Mar 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/03/18/electricite-apres-trois-ans-dattente-le-maroc-debloque-enfin-lautoproduction-1645705/)
- Médias24 refers to "Énergies renouvelables : un projet de décret ouvre la voie à leur intégration en moyenne et basse tension", 26 Jun 2026. This concerns a draft decree (likely under Law 13-09), not a BT surplus tariff — [Médias24, 6 Nov 2026](https://medias24.com/2026/11/06/du-ministere-de-la-transition-energetique-au-ministere-de-lenergie-quelles-priorites-pour-la-transition-1788099/) (search excerpt only)

### Inferences
- For a Tangier villa on BT in Nov 2026, the realistic surplus value is 0 MAD/kWh: no BT tariff and no confirmed metering or compensation mechanism. 0.18–0.21 MAD/kWh is at most a hypothesis for after a BT decision, which is expected no earlier than the next regulatory period from 1 Mar 2027. A "0.85 MAD/kWh" credit has no basis in any source found and should be removed or flagged.
- The 20% cap is measured against annual production, not consumption.

### Gaps
- I did not check ANRE's decisions page for a decision after Feb 2026. The ANRE home page still lists the Feb 2026 surplus decision among its news. Whether a BT decision came out in Oct–Nov 2026 is not fully ruled out.

## Q5. Storage (art. 15) and the storage decree

### Takeaway
Art. 15 allows a self-producer to build storage and use storage services "conformément aux conditions fixées par voie réglementaire". No published storage decree was found as of Nov 2026. Sources from mid-2026 describe it as still being finalised.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 15 text; art. 2 defines "stockage de l'énergie" as collecting the energy produced by the self-production installation for later use — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- LEXT (May 2026): art. 15 "ouvre la possibilité ... selon des conditions fixées par voie réglementaire"; storage is described as a future ("à terme") optimisation — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- "Les modalités détaillées seront précisées par un décret dédié encore en cours de finalisation" — [Wevmade, Jun 2026](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026)

### Inferences
- Behind-the-meter batteries charged from the PV are legally consistent with art. 15. There is no specific procedure yet, so declare them as part of the installation's description. Any battery added later is a modification that needs the operator's prior consent (art. 9).

### Gaps
- I could not open the 18 June 2026 Médias24 article cited in the brief. No source found confirms a storage decree after June 2026.

## Q6. Fines (arts. 28–31)

### Takeaway
Confirmed from the law text. Art. 28 fines 2,000–5,000 MAD for building, operating or increasing the power of an installation without the art. 3/4 declaration. Art. 29 fines 100,000–1,000,000 MAD for doing so without the required connection agreement or authorisation. The study should also mention art. 30 (same range, for modifying without the prior consent required by art. 9) and art. 33 (regularisation of existing installations).

### Cited Findings
- Art. 28: 2,000–5,000 MAD "quiconque réalise, exploite ou augmente la puissance ... sans procéder aux déclarations prévues aux articles 3 et 4". Art. 29: 100,000–1,000,000 MAD without the agreement or authorisation of arts. 5–6. Art. 30: 100,000–1,000,000 MAD for breaching art. 9 (unapproved modifications). Art. 31: 10,000–100,000 MAD for refusing inspectors access. Art. 25: formal notice, then an operating stop. Art. 32: the operator may cut injection or the connection — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- Art. 33: operators of pre-existing installations must apply for regularisation within 18 months of the law's entry into force — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf). Installers put the deadline at about Dec 2027, counting from 9 Jun 2026 — [Wevmade](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026)

### Inferences
- Art. 30 matters for a villa: adding panels or a battery later without the operator's prior consent is fined at the 100k–1M level, not 2k–5k.
- The start date of the 18-month art. 33 period is debatable: from the law (2023) or from the decree's entry into force (Jun 2026). Art. 36 suggests the latter for provisions that need implementing texts. No authoritative confirmation found.

### Gaps
- No case law or enforcement data found.

## Q7. Who handles declarations in Tangier (Amendis or SRM-TTA), and forms, fees, deadlines, smart meter

### Takeaway
SRM Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceïma (SRM-TTA) has been operational since early Sept 2025. One aggregator says Amendis continues to operate until its delegated-management contract expires at the end of 2026, so who handles declarations in Nov 2026 is still in transition. The legal addressee is "le gestionnaire du réseau de distribution concerné" (art. 4), so the applicant should ask SRM-TTA first and check whether Amendis' customer agencies still take files. The smart meter is mandatory for grid-connected installations and only the network operator may install it (arts. 18–19). No published forms or fee schedule were found for BT declarations.

### Cited Findings
- SRM-TTA announced the start of operations on 3 Sep 2025 — [Le Desk](https://ledesk.ma/encontinu/lancement-officiel-de-la-societe-regionale-multiservices-tanger-tetouan-al-hoceima/) (search excerpt); [SRM-TTA site](https://srm-tta.ma/)
- "Depuis le 1er septembre 2025, la SRM-TTA est opérationnelle mais Amendis poursuit son exploitation jusqu'à l'expiration du contrat fin 2026"; the contract covers 2002–2026 — [Kherba (aggregator, lower reliability)](https://kherba.com/regies/amendis)
- An installer lists "SRM Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceïma — Anciennement AMENDIS — gestionnaire BT/MT région Nord" — [Wevmade](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026)
- Connected <11 kW: no fees, and the SRM checks hosting capacity on a first-come-first-served basis — [Médias24](https://medias24.com/2026/03/18/electricite-apres-trois-ans-dattente-le-maroc-debloque-enfin-lautoproduction-1645705/)
- Arts. 18–19: smart meter required for billing, except for off-grid (art. 3) installations, and installed only by the network operator. Art. 20: ANRE sets minimum metering conditions and how surplus is determined. Art. 21: the administration is to digitalise procedures — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- Installer claim: decree 2.24.804 (Sept 2024) makes a bidirectional smart meter mandatory — [Wevmade](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026) (unverified; no other source)
- The application file must include proof of title to, or the right to use, the building or installation (decree arts. 1, 3, 9) — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)

### Inferences
- Practical sequence for a villa below 11 kW: (1) file the declaration with the BT network operator (SRM-TTA, or Amendis while the hand-over is under way) before works start, with title deed, single-line diagram and equipment specs; (2) the operator checks and reserves hosting capacity; (3) sign the connection convention; (4) install; (5) the operator installs the smart meter; (6) file for operation with proof of ownership (art. 9). Whether meter costs are charged to the customer is not documented.

### Gaps
- No SRM-TTA or Amendis web page, form, fee or response deadline for autoproduction declarations was found. The srm-tta.ma home page was not scraped in detail.
- The exact date Amendis hands over (end of contract 2026) is not confirmed by a primary source.

## Q8. BT inverter grid-code rules: single-phase injection limits, phase unbalance

### Takeaway
No Moroccan BT grid code, ANRE technical regulation, or ONEE/SRM/Amendis specification on single-phase injection limits or phase unbalance for three-phase BT connections was found. The law requires conformity with national or international standards (art. 17). The decree refers to network codes and technical regulations "approuvés", and to the CRENT (transmission) and MT distribution specifications, not to BT.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 17: installations to be connected "doivent être conformes aux normes nationales ou internationales adoptées" — [Law 82-21](https://anre.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Loi-82-21-BO_7400_Fr.pdf)
- Decree art. 27: the technical study follows the operator's specifications, technical regulations and approved network codes, assesses "contraintes de charge et de baisse de tension", and sets operating parameters. Where network access is involved, the CRENT or the MT distribution specifications apply — [LEXT](https://www.lext.fr/fr/actualite-juridique/maroc-autoproduction-publication-du-decret-n-2-25-100)
- ANRE says the BT tariff awaits "la mise en place du cadre réglementaire et technique adéquat" — [ANRE](https://anre.ma/actualites/lanre-fixe-le-tarif-de-lexcedent-de-lenergie-electrique-pour-la-periode-de-regulation-allant-du-1er-mars-2026-au-28-fevrier-2027/)
- Installer list of BT technical conditions: anti-islanding (IEC 62116), THD per IEC standards, approved equipment, differential protection, signed single-line diagram — [Wevmade](https://www.wevmade.com/blog/loi-82-21-autoproduction-injection-bt-maroc-2026) (installer, not official)

### Inferences
- The BT technical framework is not yet in place, which ANRE itself acknowledges. Any single-phase limit, for example a cap per phase, would currently be set case by case by the SRM in the connection convention. A balanced three-phase inverter is the low-risk choice for a three-phase villa.

### Gaps
- No ONEE, SRM or Amendis BT generator connection specification was found. This needs a direct request to SRM-TTA.

## Q9. VAT on solar equipment: DGI letters, Finance Laws 2025/2026, 0% invoicing

### Takeaway
DGI letter of 7 Jul 2022 (reply to CGEM Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceïma): photovoltaic panels are at the reduced 10% rate. A turnkey solution with installation sold at a single global price is at 20% on the whole price. Panels sold separately from installation stay at 10%, and the installation service is at 20%. An earlier DGI letter of 29 Apr 2021 (to AMISOLE) applied the LF 2021 exemption without deduction right to panels sold as-is. Barlamane (Jul 2026) still describes PV panels at 10%. No change in LF 2025 or LF 2026 was confirmed: the CGI 2026 query timed out. There is no legal basis for a residential installer to charge 0% VAT on a turnkey villa system.

### Cited Findings
- DGI correspondence dated 7 Jul 2022, in reply to CGEM (region president Adil Rais): "les panneaux photovoltaïques sont soumis à la TVA au taux réduit de 10%"; turnkey solution with installation at a single global price "soumise à la TVA au taux normal de 20%", taxable base = global price; if panels are sold separately from installation, panels at 10% and installation at 20% — [Portail Sud Maroc, relaying Le360, 18 Jul 2022](https://portailsudmaroc.com/actualite/21904/traitement-fiscal-des-installations-de-panneaux-photovoltaques-les-prc)
- DGI letter of 29 Apr 2021 to AMISOLE/FENELEC: sale as-is of panels "exonérée de la TVA sans droit à déduction" (LF 2021 regime). Sale and installation of a full PV system at 20%, even if component prices are itemised, when done under a single global contract. Turnkey PV under the investment-goods exemption (art. 92-I-6° CGI) is possible only for eligible taxpayer businesses — [Les Inspirations Éco, 5 May 2021](https://leseco.ma/maroc/tva-sur-les-equipements-solaires-la-dgi-tranche-le-traitement-fiscal-a-appliquer.html)
- Mid-2026: "Les panneaux photovoltaïques et les chauffe-eau solaires supportent une TVA de 10 %"; components used to manufacture panels (cells, solar glass, frames, structures, junction boxes) are exempt with deduction right — [Barlamane, 1 Jul 2026](https://barlamane.com/fr/le-maroc-ouvre-lautoproduction-solaire-aux-entreprises-plafonne-les-injections-a-20-et-maintient-en-attente-le-regime-residentiel-de-basse-tension/)
- Gide note on the LF 2021 exemption being counter-productive — [Gide](https://www.gide.com/news-insights/panneaux-photovoltaiques-et-chauffe-eaux-solaires-lexoneration-de-tva-contre-productive/) (search excerpt)
- In 2026 Morocco has only two VAT rates, 20% and 10%, after the reform begun in LF 2024 — [Upsilon Consulting](https://www.upsilon-consulting.com/tva-au-maroc/) (search excerpt)

### Inferences
- The study's claim (10% panels alone, 20% turnkey) matches the 7 Jul 2022 DGI letter and recent press. A 0% invoice to a private homeowner for a turnkey system is not legitimate: the investment-goods exemptions (art. 92-I-6°, art. 123-22°) apply only to VAT-registered businesses buying fixed assets. A 0% line could at most appear if the installer wrongly applies the old LF 2021 exemption, which no longer applies to panels. Inverters, batteries and structures sold to individuals fall under the standard 20% rate unless separately listed in art. 99. Separating the panel supply contract (10%) from the installation contract (20%) is allowed by the DGI and slightly reduces the VAT bill.
